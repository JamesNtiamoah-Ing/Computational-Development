Power-to-Liquid (PtL) Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) Simulation
📌 Project Overview
This project contains a rigorous steady-state techno-economic analysis (TEA) and Aspen Plus flowsheet simulation of a syngas-to-SAF pilot facility. The model evaluates thermodynamic limitations, carbon capture efficiency, and commercial scaling feasibility for converting green hydrogen and captured CO₂ into synthetic aviation hydrocarbons (n-dodecane proxy).

Thermodynamic Framework & Methodology
* **Property Package:** Non-Random Two-Liquid (NRTL) was selected to compute phase equilibria for highly polar aqueous mixtures and wastewater byproducts.
* **Supercritical Components:** H₂, O₂, CO, and CO₂ were specified as Henry’s Law components to accurately model trace supercritical solubilities within liquid phases.
* **Convergence Strategy:** Resolved mass accumulation and Vapor-Liquid-Liquid Equilibrium (VLLE) deadlocks in the closed-loop recycle system using a multivariable Broyden simultaneous convergence block with a strict 0.0001 X-tolerance.

Process Flow & Unit Operations
1. **Green Hydrogen Production:** Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM) electrolyzer modeled as an isothermal stoichiometric reactor (70°C, 40 bar).
2. **Syngas Generation:** Reverse Water-Gas Shift (RWGS) equilibrium reactor (800°C) favoring endothermic CO production.
3. **Fischer-Tropsch Synthesis:** Low-Temperature Fischer-Tropsch (LTFT) catalytic reactor (220°C, 25 bar) optimized to suppress methane over-cracking and maximize heavy distillate yield.
4. **Product Recovery:** A 30-stage rigorous fractional distillation column (RadFrac) operating at 1 bar to isolate the aviation-grade fraction.
5. **Circular Integration:** Features a continuous, multivariable-optimized unreacted tail-gas recycle loop with an exact 5% purge fraction to prevent inert gas accumulation.

Key Simulation Results
* **Production Yield:** Achieved a steady-state SAF yield of 31.31 kg/hr.
* **Purity:** Concentrated final proxy product (n-dodecane) to an industrial purity of 99.97% by mass.
* **Carbon Efficiency:** The integrated tail-gas loop successfully elevated total carbon atom efficiency to 97.07%.
* **Heat Integration:** Pinch analysis via Aspen Energy Analyzer identified a 60.51% reduction in thermal utility demand through process-to-process heat recovery.
* **Techno-Economic Scale-Up:** A Python financial model utilizing the Six-Tenths capacity rule projected that scaling the facility 100x reduces the Levelized Cost of Fuel (LCOF) from a pilot baseline of $9.59/kg to a highly competitive $1.67/kg.
